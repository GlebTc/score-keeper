Voice and screen

On iPhone and iPad, leave this off and tap the mic once per point. Commands are in English.

Voice commands
  • “Red plus 1”, “blue point”, “add 2 to red”. “Red plus 21” adds 21
  • “Red score 21” sets Red’s score to 21
  • “Left score plus 1” and “right plus 1” score the team on that side
  • Rough English still counts. “Reed plus won” is taken as red plus 1, and the guess is shown so you can undo it.
  • “Blue team minus 1” removes a point, even if you also say “point”
  • “Undo”
  • “Blue serve” or “blue is serving” lights up that team. Scoring a point does this too
  • “What’s the score?”, “tell me the score”, “read the score”, “who’s winning?”
  • When one more point wins the set, the app says “Game point” out loud
  • “Red wins the set”
  • “Restart the score” clears the points and keeps the sets
  • “Restart the game”, then “yes”, starts the match over
  • “Left color green”, “switch blue team to yellow”, “make the right team gold”
  • “Volleyball”, “play volleyball”, “table tennis”, “first to 21”, “free play”
  • “Play to 15”, “first to 15”, “first to 2 sets”, “win by 2”, “no win by 2”
  • “Switch sides”, “switch the sides”, “rename left to Hawks”
  • “Left green”, “make the left team green”, “change the color of the right team to gold”
  • “Sound off”, “keep the screen on”, “how many sets?”, “nobody serving”
  • “Stop” turns the mic off

Keys: 1 and 2 add a point, Shift+1 and Shift+2 remove one, U undoes, M or Space toggles the mic.

On iPhone or iPad: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. After it has loaded once, it also works offline.

Scores stay on this device. Chrome, Edge, and Safari can hear commands. Use the buttons or the text box in other browsers.
